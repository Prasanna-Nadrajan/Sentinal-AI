SentinelAI Prototype Demo Walkthrough

Estimated Duration: 3.5 - 4.5 minutes
Pacing: Conversational, guiding the viewer's eyes to specific parts of the screen. Wait a beat after clicking buttons.

---

1. Dashboard Introduction (0:00 - 0:45)
(Visual: Full screen of the SentinelAI dashboard, no prediction run yet.)

Speaker:
"Welcome to the SentinelAI prototype. This is the primary clinical dashboard a triage nurse or doctor would use. 

I designed this with a dark mode aesthetic specifically to reduce eye strain during long ICU night shifts. 

On the left, we have the Patient Vitals panel, where real-time data from Electronic Health Records flows in. On the right is our analytical panel—currently empty—where our AI will display the Sepsis Probability and its explanations once we run a prediction."

---

2. High-Risk Sepsis Case (0:45 - 1:45)
(Visual: Zoom in slightly on the 'Patient Vitals' form.)

Speaker:
"Let's simulate a high-risk case. The Vitals panel captures standard metrics like Heart Rate and Temperature, plus critical values like MAP (Mean Arterial Pressure) and WBC (White Blood Cell count).

Here, the Heart Rate is elevated at 110, and MAP is dangerously low at 62. Let's click 'Run Sepsis Prediction'."

(Action: Click 'Run Sepsis Prediction'.)

Speaker:
"Instantly, the backend processes the data. The Sepsis Probability Gauge on the right animates up to an 85% severe risk. 

Because it crosses our 70% threshold, the UI flags this patient with a red, pulsing 'CRITICAL' badge. This tells the doctor immediately that urgent attention is needed."

---

3. Explainable AI Breakdown (1:45 - 2:45)
(Visual: Focus on the 'InsightCard' panel below the gauge.)

Speaker:
"But a high score isn't enough; the doctor needs to know why. Below the gauge is our Clinical Assessment panel, powered by Explainable AI (SHAP and LIME).

The Feature Attribution section shows the AI's logic. It highlights the high Heart Rate and the low MAP. The AI is explicitly saying: 'I am flagging this patient because their blood pressure is collapsing while their heart rate is spiking.' 

This eliminates the 'Black Box' problem. The system also maps these findings to hospital protocols, recommending vasopressor support for the low MAP."

---

4. Low-Risk Stable Case (2:45 - 3:45)
(Visual: Reset Vitals to normal: HR 75, Temp 37.0, MAP 90, WBC 7.0.)

Speaker:
"It's equally important to see how the system handles a stable patient to avoid 'alarm fatigue'.

Let me adjust these vitals to a healthy baseline. We'll drop Heart Rate to 75 and raise MAP to 90. I'll click 'Run Sepsis Prediction' again."

(Action: Click predict. Gauge drops to ~12%.)

Speaker:
"The gauge stays low at 12%, and we get a green 'STABLE' badge. 

Our AI explains that because all vitals are within healthy baselines, it confidently suppresses the sepsis risk score. There are no frantic alerts—just a quiet confirmation of stability."

---

5. PDF Reporting (3:45 - 4:30)
(Visual: Move cursor to 'Download Clinical Report' button in the header and click it.)

Speaker:
"Finally, clinical documentation is vital. By clicking 'Download Clinical Report', our backend uses ReportLab to instantly generate a print-ready PDF. 

It packages the vitals, the risk score, and the AI's feature attributions into an industry-standard medical document, ready for the patient's chart.

That is SentinelAI: predicting risk, explaining its logic, and integrating seamlessly into clinical workflows."
